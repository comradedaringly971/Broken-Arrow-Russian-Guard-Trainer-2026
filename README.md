## 🎮 About the Game

Broken Arrow: Russian Guard is a strategic real-time modern warfare tactics DLC developed by Steel Balalaika and published by Slitherine. It released on August 31, 2026, and requires the base game Broken Arrow. The expansion adds a Russian Guard specialization, new infantry squads, armored vehicles, artillery, air defense, aircraft, two maps, two scenarios, and a dedicated AI army.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Broken Arrow: Russian Guard**. This trainer enhances offline Broken Arrow: Russian Guard gameplay by modifying deployment resources, unit survivability, ammunition, fuel, ability cooldowns, and artillery support. It is intended for campaign missions, scenarios, and skirmishes against AI while preserving the game's real-time command, line-of-sight, armor penetration, deck-building, and resupply systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with Russian Guard battlegroups, test unit loadouts, practice combined-arms tactics, explore the Daugavpils and Coal Mountain maps, or replay missions with reduced resource pressure.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Select units and issue standard movement orders such as attack move, fast move, reverse move, or return to base. Fast movement prioritizes roads and speed, while reverse movement helps armored vehicles retreat without exposing weaker rear armor. The trainer's mobility option is best used for testing formations and repositioning units rather than compatibility terrain or line-of-sight rules.

### Tools & Inventory

Broken Arrow does not use a traditional character inventory. Unit equipment is configured through the Armory and Army Builder, where vehicles and combat units can receive different ammunition and armor configurations. During missions, ammunition, fuel, supplies, smoke, decoys, and active protection charges are battlefield resources. The trainer can maintain these resources for uninterrupted testing.

### World & Environment

The Russian Guard expansion adds the Daugavpils and Coal Mountain maps, two dedicated scenarios, and a new AI army for Skirmish mode. Use the trainer in offline content to examine urban approaches, elevated industrial positions, artillery lanes, defensive zones, and the interaction between reconnaissance, armor, infantry, air support, and air defense.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat depends on line of sight, weapon range, armor facing, kinetic penetration, shaped-charge penetration, suppression, ammunition type, and unit abilities. Use artillery for explosive, smoke, or precision fire missions, combine reconnaissance with direct and indirect fire, and protect aircraft with air defense and defensive abilities. Trainer options can remove resource and cooldown limits, but positioning and target selection still determine battlefield effectiveness.
